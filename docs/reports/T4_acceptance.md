# T4 source verification draft

The owner authorized four T4 designs, ArF dry equipment/resist, a separate 3×3×5 G2 preparation machine, a 2×2×3 G2 packager, and high binning from T3 logic/compute to T4. This supersedes the earlier T3-only/high-binning hold. Logic remains capped at eventual T5; compute core choices remain deferred until T6.

## Resulting behavior

The preparation machine accepts only fresh trusted PURE wafers and four Silicon, consumes the original carrier once, and returns that carrier with G2 preparation. Preparation remains distinct from substrate; legacy lots default G1, mixed/refilled lots cannot inherit preparation, and private roll/identity data remain server-only. The 1,200-tick job freezes its 1,000 FE/t cost, pauses for power/structure/output, persists, and recovers the unprepared carrier once on break.

Both new multiblocks use lower-front controllers, mandatory upper operating panels, any horizontal facing/front column, one or two freely placed FE ports and joined casing textures. The controller owns all inventory/FE/job data. Power handlers revalidate ownership and actual world identity on every operation. Lower cabinets expose status, upper panels expose operational slots. Stale/closed menus cannot change mode; mode locks during an active queue.

The E-beam writer now selects eleven designs, including all four T4 recipes. Existing lithography reads actual preparation generation from the trusted lot and pins it in saved contracts. G2 packaging handles completed T4 lots; G1 refuses them. T3 logic/compute high binning targets registered T4 items at 0.15 times the source frozen yield, doubles time and FE/t, and scraps failures. It reuses calibrated private cumulative rolls and freezes judgments, target, quantities and costs at admission. No T5 item or T6 gameplay is added. Six equipment/resist recipes and five structure recipes consume prior-generation logic; four E-beam recipes are separately editable JSON.
